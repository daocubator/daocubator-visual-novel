# Koraba — The Creator

**Role:** Visionary creator, musician, and community builder (Primary POV protagonist in Episodic Mode).
**Voice:** Grounded, soulful, passionate about real human impact; speaks in rhythms and tangible truths ("Music, medicine, and people on the ground—that's the actual treasury").
**Gear & Visual Motif:** Musician and bridge keeper with dark locs, a layered blue wrap, beads, and a kora. His 2026 character sheet uses hand-etched black/umber contours, dense hatch and stipple, antique ivory/ochre paper, and a grounded ceremonial silhouette. Keep its spiral, star, and orbit motifs in the textile and framing language.
**Want:** To build sovereign creative sanctuaries (like *Essential Food and Medicine*) that distribute real nourishment—music, art, free health juices, and transformative community trips—without begging gatekeepers.
**Need:** To claim digital and legal sovereignty over his own keys, agreements, and payouts instead of outsourcing the "boring admin stuff" to people who haven't earned that trust.
**Flaw:** Trusts too easily in the name of harmony; wants to focus purely on creating and healing while letting someone else hold the digital keys ("No keys, no cheese").
**Lessons he embodies:** [[No Keys No Cheese]], [[The One Dollar Test Transaction]], [[Creator vs Administrator Gatekeeping]], [[Make and Enforce Agreements]]

## Real-World Inspiration (Asya's Narrative)

Koraba represents the visionary creators in collectives like *Essential Food and Medicine* who bring all the actual labor, art, music, and community programs (distributing free health juices, organizing sanctuary trips to Brazil and South Dakota, building interactive showcases for Lisbon), only to find themselves stranded or blocked when administrators hoard the multi-sig keys or let awe curdle into jealousy.

## Arc across the Constellation Episodes

- **Episode 1 — "No Keys, No Cheese" (POV: Koraba):** Confronted with the nightmare of being stranded in Brazil while $60,000 vanishes and Clara holds the keys, Koraba rewinds time to rotate leaked keys on-chain, vet incoming DAO administrators, and enforce the $1 test transaction rule.
- **Episode 2 — "Frozen Pay, Empty Stage" (POV: Xochit & Koraba):** After working sleepless weeks on a game and music showcase for Lisbon, Koraba faces an administrative payout freeze from a jealous gatekeeper—and learns how co-op solidarity and objective milestone escrow defeat ego trips.
- **Episode 3 — "Blood, Cacao & Paper" (POV: Koraba & Xochit):** Learns why counter-culture collectives still need written vesting agreements, legal wrappers, and capped recoupment deals.
- **Episode 4 — "One Tired Click Drained the Apothecary" (Co-star):** Holds the line as a co-signer alongside Xochit when Clara is tempted by whale money and spoofed transactions.
