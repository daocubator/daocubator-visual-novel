# Vetting DAO Administrators

**Principle (popup line 1):** Never vote a stranger onto your multisig or treasury council on "good vibes" alone—verify their history and scope their permissions first.
**Failure mode:** Fast-moving collectives frequently rubber-stamp charismatic newcomers into administrative or signing roles without checking their prior DAOs, on-chain wallet history, or signing written contributor agreements.
**Real case:** **Build Finance DAO ($470k) & Wonderland / Sifu Reveal (2022)** — Unvetted administrators and pseudonymous treasury managers with histories of failed or fraudulent projects were handed minting and treasury keys, leading to insider drains and catastrophic collapses of trust.
**Mitigation:** Conduct due diligence on past collectives and on-chain wallet clusters, require a written contributor agreement upfront, and use scoped role permissions (e.g., 90-day trial with proposal-drafting rights, never instant root treasury keys).
**In the game:** Episode 1, Decision 2 — Clara nominates a smooth-talking co-administrator to "speed up payouts" before the Brazil trip. Rubber-stamping the vote leads to an insider treasury drain; pausing to research their wallet history exposes two drained DAOs in their past.
