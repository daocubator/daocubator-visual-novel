# Make and Enforce Agreements

**Principle (popup line 1):** Counter-culture values and fast innovation are no substitute for written agreements, clear deliverables, and legal armor.
**Failure mode:** Believing that written contracts, vesting schedules, and legal entities are "corporate poison" that ruin spiritual or creative vibes—leaving the collective defenseless against dead equity, predatory 360 deals, and personal regulator liability.
**Real case:** **Wasserman's Founder's Dilemmas, CFTC v. Ooki DAO ($634k), & Early Creative Collectives** — Collectives that skipped written founder agreements and legal wrappers suffered 3× higher breakup rates, lost 50% of their cap tables to departing members, and saw individual contributors held personally liable as general partners.
**Mitigation:** Put every agreement in plain, enforceable writing on day one: 4-year vesting with a 1-year cliff, a real legal wrapper (Swiss Verein or Cooperative LLC), and capped, non-cross-collateralized distribution contracts.
**In the game:** Episode 3 — Ash El and Xochtl reject the "handshake vibes only" trap and paper their co-op agreements with Vera before launching.
