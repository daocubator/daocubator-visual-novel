# Co-op Solidarity (Winning Together)

**Principle (popup line 1):** A true cooperative wins together by sharing both the shield and the harvest—not by pitting solo stars against solo bosses.
**Failure mode:** Treating a collective like either a lawless free-for-all or a disguised dictatorship where one founder or admin hoards power while creators burn out in isolation.
**Real case:** **Rochdale Principles & Mondragon / Essential Food & Medicine Co-op Lessons** — A century of cooperative history and modern DAO post-mortems show that collectives survive when members practice mutual aid, transparent accounting, rotating stewardship, and collective defense against capture.
**Mitigation:** Pair creative autonomy with shared co-op stewardship: peer sign-offs, transparent treasuries, mutual defense when a member is mistreated, and planned rest rotations.
**In the game:** Episode 2, Decision 2 & 3 — Xochtl and Ash El use co-op solidarity rather than destructive public infighting to unlock the Lisbon showcase and replace solo admin gatekeeping with rotating council stewardship.
