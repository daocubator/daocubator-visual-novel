# Creator vs Administrator Gatekeeping

**Principle (popup line 1):** When non-creators hold unilateral payout power over creators, admiration can curdle into jealousy and gatekeeping.
**Failure mode:** Creators bring all the tangible labor, art, code, and community value, while administrators hold the proposal levers and multisig keys. Without objective milestone agreements, an insecure administrator can hold a creator's payout hostage over personal ego.
**Real case:** **The Lisbon Showcase Incident (The "Amy & Edward / Jena" Story)** — A talented creator ("Edward" / Ash El) built an entire interactive game for a collective's presentation in Lisbon. The administrator ("Amy" / Lay-Lay), overwhelmed by jealousy of his talent, flat-out refused to approve his payout. Teammate "Jena" (Xochtl) proved the bias by having the creator submit the exact same work under a different avatar—whereupon the administrator approved the payout instantly.
**Mitigation:** Separate administrative operations from subjective creative veto: use objective milestone-based escrow, peer co-op review, and blind/neutral deliverable verification so personal ego can never freeze a contributor's livelihood.
**In the game:** Episode 2 — Lay-Lay freezes Ash El's payout hours before the Lisbon presentation. Xochtl's covert neutral-avatar submission exposes the bias on-chain and triggers constitutional reform.
