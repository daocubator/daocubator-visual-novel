Staging & Composition Constraints

  • Ren'Py Auto-Naming: Files in game/images/ must be named bg <id>.png (with a space) to match scene bg <id>.
  • Sprite Clearance: Upper 2/3 of frame holds waist-up 3D polished 24k gold character sculptures at xalign 0.18 (left), 0.50 (center), and 0.82 (right).
  • Textbox Overlay: Bottom 277px is covered by dark-glass game/gui/textbox.png.
  • Value Contrast: Mid-ground needs controlled value contrast so bright specular gold silhouettes read cleanly without visual clutter.
