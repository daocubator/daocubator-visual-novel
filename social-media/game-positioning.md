# Game Positioning & Description

Working marketing language for the game. Lead with creative collectives and startups, which are familiar to a broad audience. Use “DAO” when speaking to crypto-native audiences or when the specific technology matters; do not make understanding that acronym a prerequisite for understanding the pitch.

## Short description

A warm, sharp visual novel about whether a creative collective can survive the choices that have broken real startups.

## Expanded description

A story-rich visual novel about a creative collective facing money, governance, security, and relationship crises. Players make consequential choices, see how shortcuts turn into disasters, and can rewind to try again. Through warm satire, players unlock good endings by learning how collectives are undone by the hard conversations they put off.

## Positioning notes

- Lead with the recognizable human story: people trying to build something together and deciding how to share power, money, risk, and responsibility.
- Describe the setting as a creative collective or startup before introducing DAOs. Explain “DAO” only when relevant to the audience or the specific piece of content.
- The distinguishing play experience is making consequential choices, seeing the fallout, and rewinding to try again.
- The tone is warm satire: candid about the failures and absurdities, while still believing in collective ownership.
- The current story documents are drafts and describe both an episodic crisis format and a longer founder story. Revisit this copy if the final release structure changes.
