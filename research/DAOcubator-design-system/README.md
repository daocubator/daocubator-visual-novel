Warm earth ground with one small gold light. DAOcubator looks like a lit circuit growing out of soil: roots below, circuit traces in the middle, a canopy or fruiting body above. Use it for the web pages, the pitch deck and the visual novel so all three read as one world.

## Content fundamentals

- **Voice:** declarative and conviction-led. Short claims, then one sentence of mechanism. "Technology is merely the infrastructure; the community is the asset."
- **The core metaphor is organic-meets-digital.** Name systems as living parts: *Bedrock, Root System, Fruiting Bodies, Canopy*, *constellation*, *nodes*, *seeds*. Keep the metaphor consistent within a page; don't mix in mechanical or military imagery.
- **Casing:** titles in sentence case ("The architecture of liberation", "Nodes of the regenerative ecosystem"). Proper names keep their own casing: Primordia, Kin DAO, EarthChange, Hyphae, Near Protocol. Category labels are UPPERCASE in `label` (CARE, CULTURE, LAND, SCALING).
- **Pattern for entity lines:** `Name: what it does`. "Kin DAO: Collective land purchase & regenerative practice."
- **Emphasis:** bold the closing phrase of a lede in `glow` ("The true currency is **human resilience.**"). No italics for emphasis, no exclamation marks, no emoji.
- **Attribution:** an en dash and the name, in `caption`: "– Asya Abdrahman".

## Color

- Dark only. Pages sit on `ink`; raised panels and cards on `char`; alternate layers on `ground`. Timber and label cells use `field`.
- Gold is a light, not a paint. `glow` is the hot core: rings, panel titles, links, emphasis phrases, the focus ring, always in small areas. Spend `ember` and `clay` for the warm body of a composition and `glow-core` only at the centre of a glow. One focal glow per composition.
- Glow or shimmer, never both. A glowing object holds its hue steady; a pulp surface drifts hue across itself (`slate`, `lavender`, `viol`) and takes no highlight.
- Cool colour is light on a warm surface: `teal` for labels and small accents, `slate` and `lavender` for pulp surfaces, `ultramarine` only pooled in recesses. Haze and distance tint toward `clay`, never grey or blue.
- Give each job one register:
  - Atmosphere and light: `ink`, `field`, `clay`, `ember`, `glow`.
  - Anything that must read at a glance (characters, interactables, interface): the `mark` on a ground that changes from instance to instance.
  - Material you get close to (surfaces, held objects, terrain): `slate`, `lavender`, `viol`, `rust`.
- Text: `flame` for titles and ledes, `bone` for paragraphs, `ash` for secondary text and captions. Use `ash` on ink, char, ground and panel only, never on `field`. Use `teal` text on ink, char and ground only, never on `panel`. Text on a `glow`, `ember` or `clay` fill is `on-glow`.
- Dividers are `rule` and `rule-strong`, decorative only. A control's border is `ash`. Structural hairlines (table grids, twine) are `sand`.

## Type

- Three families. `serif` (Fraunces, light 300) for display, titles, quotes and the hub name. `sans` (Karla) for everything you read in paragraphs. `mono` (IBM Plex Mono) for `label`: uppercase, `letterSpacing` 0.2em, weight 500.
- Headlines: `display` for heroes, `title` for slide and section titles, `heading` below that. Keep display weights light; the elegance comes from thin strokes on dark.
- `lede` in `flame` above `body-lg` in `bone` is the statement pattern (see PullQuote).
- Panel titles are `sans` `panel-title` in `glow`, not serif.
- Fraunces, Karla and IBM Plex Mono are open-source (SIL OFL), bundled as Latin-subset woff2 files in `fonts/`. Fraunces keeps its optical-size axis, so large display sizes get the high-contrast cut automatically.

## Layout, frames and light

- Structure stays square (`radius-0`). Round shapes are rings, discs, seed dots and the knots on twine (`radius-full`).
- Sequence over symmetry: nothing is centred or mirrored, panels vary in width and group unevenly, sheets are pinned slightly out of true.
- The aperture is the level grammar: a bounded, brighter opening inside a darker field. Use it for doors, goals and transitions. A dark horizontal band (`ink`) sits low in the frame as the ground line, with tall narrow panels (`ground`) beside it on a shared horizon.
- Hold things up, don't box them in. A HungPanel hangs a `panel` on visible `sand` twine inside an oversized `field` timber frame, with wall showing on every side. Keep the twine visible and the ends long. Use it for dialogue boxes, portraits, inventory slots and menu panels. Plain `Panel` is for stacked layers.
- The mark: one glossy near-black element in three scales, a stroke, a small solid and a timber member. Ground varies per instance, `mark` stays constant. It enters obliquely, crosses off-centre and exits past the edge. Detached squares and lozenges in `mark` are status pips, counters and rank marks.
- No drop shadows. Light comes from glow: `glow-sm` on node rings and seed dots, `glow-lg` on the single focal point.
- Slide margins `space-6`; hero margins `space-7`. Titles sit top-left; illustration takes the right half or the centre.
- Focus ring: 2px solid `glow`, offset 2px.

## Imagery

- Warm illustration and photography from Asya Abdrahman's practice: paintings that glow from a core, paper reliefs, glossy marks on iridescent grounds. Roots become circuit traces become canopy, read bottom to top: analog community (roots), DAO ledger (circuits), physical flourishing (canopy, villages).
- Surfaces carry a history. Treat soaked, run, scorched, charred and abraded as authored events applied in sequence and left visible. Fire is small and sited: dozens of tiny flames, 0.1 to 0.5 percent of the frame, lit where combustible material is embedded.
- The frames in the **Imagery** asset group come from the earlier slate-and-gold direction. Use them for composition only; take colour from the palette above.
- Fine light dust in `glow-core` is allowed as atmosphere, sparingly.

## Iconography

- Icons are thin line glyphs (hands with heart, palette, house with sprout, network) set inside a double ring: `glow` outer, `ember` inner (see NodeCard). Stroke about 1.5px in `glow`.
- No icon set was supplied; draw icons as single-weight line SVGs in that style. Flagged: pick or commission a set before production.
