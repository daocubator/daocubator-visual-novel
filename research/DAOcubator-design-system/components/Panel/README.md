A raised content panel with a glow sans title, used for layered explanations (the "architecture of liberation" stack).

- Provide `title` (a `panel-title` line in `glow`) and body text as children.
- Stack panels with no gap for a layered list; adjacent panels get a `rule-strong` divider. Alternate `tone="deep"` (`ground`) to separate layers.
- Use `framed` when a panel floats on an illustration and needs a `rule-strong` edge.
- Do: keep body to two or three lines. Don't: put serif headings or icons inside; that's NodeCard's job. For a dialogue box, portrait or inventory slot, use HungPanel instead.
