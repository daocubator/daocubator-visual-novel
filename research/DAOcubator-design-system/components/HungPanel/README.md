A panel hung on knotted `sand` twine inside an oversized `field` timber frame, with the `char` wall showing on every side. The container holds a thing up rather than boxing it in.

- Provide `title` (a `panel-title` line in `glow`) and body text as children.
- Use for dialogue boxes, portraits, inventory slots and menu panels. One or two per composition.
- Do: keep body to two or three lines, leave the panel off-centre in the frame, keep the twine visible and the loose ends long. Don't: trim the twine, centre the panel, or nest one hung panel in another.
- Needs space below it for the dangling ends. For plain stacked layers, use Panel.
